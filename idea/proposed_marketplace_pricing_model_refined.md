# Proposed Marketplace Pricing Model — Refined Version

## 1. Pricing Strategy

The marketplace should not try to copy Auto Trader's pricing model.

The initial commercial strategy should be built around:

- Very low friction for private sellers
- Strong free functionality
- Simple, transparent pricing
- No vehicle-value price bands
- Paid visibility rather than paywalled basic functionality
- Dealer subscriptions as the main recurring revenue stream
- Seller intelligence and analytics as premium value
- Optional boosts and promotions
- Future referral and data products

The core principle is:

> **Charge for additional visibility, intelligence and commercial advantage — not for basic marketplace access.**

---

## 2. Competitive Positioning

The marketplace cannot initially compete with Auto Trader on audience size.

Auto Trader can charge relatively high listing fees because it already has a very large buyer audience.

A new marketplace needs a different proposition.

The seller should be able to think:

> **There is no downside to listing my car here as well.**

That makes a strong free tier strategically important.

The initial proposition should therefore be:

```text
List for free
        ↓
Pay only if you want more visibility
        ↓
Pay more if you want maximum visibility and selling intelligence
```

## 3. Recommended Private Seller Pricing

### Free

**£0**

Duration:

```text
30 days
```

Includes:

- Full vehicle advert
- All supported photos
- Vehicle specification
- Equipment
- Description
- Buyer messaging
- Likes and watchlist visibility
- Basic seller analytics
- AI advert creation
- AI advert improvement suggestions
- Basic price guidance
- Advert quality/completeness score
- Standard search visibility
- Standard Discover visibility

The Free package should be a genuinely complete advert.

It should not be deliberately weakened to force payment.

### Why Free Should Be Strong

Better free adverts improve the whole marketplace:

```text
Better adverts
    ↓
Better buyer experience
    ↓
More engagement
    ↓
More enquiries
    ↓
More successful sellers
    ↓
More listings
```

Features such as AI advert generation, basic pricing guidance and advert quality checks should therefore remain available to free sellers.

These tools improve marketplace quality.

## 7. Key Difference vs Auto Trader

The marketplace should avoid charging based on vehicle value.

The same advert pricing should apply whether the car is worth:

```text
£2,000
£20,000
£50,000
£100,000
```

Benefits:

- Easy to understand
- Easy to advertise
- No arbitrary vehicle-value bands
- Sellers can change price without changing advert package
- Easier checkout
- Easier customer support
- Easier billing implementation

Potential competitive message:

> **One fair price, whatever your car is worth.**

Another option:

> **Selling a £50,000 car shouldn't cost more just because the car costs more.**

## 10. Boost Philosophy

A boost should improve visibility without destroying buyer relevance.

Conceptual ranking:

```text
Search relevance
+
Buyer/vehicle match
+
Listing quality
+
Freshness
+
Distance
+
Organic engagement
+
Paid visibility weighting
```

Paid placement should never completely override buyer criteria.

For example:

```text
Buyer searches:
BMW M4
Under £50,000
Within 100 miles
```

A boosted unrelated vehicle should never appear simply because the seller paid more.

Buyer trust is more valuable than short-term boost revenue.

## 15. Dealer Pricing Strategy

Dealers should become the main recurring-revenue customer group.

The marketplace should use simple, transparent SaaS-style pricing.

Recommended starting point:

```text
Starter
£49/month

Pro
£149/month

Unlimited
£299/month
```

Unlike a bespoke quote model, the dealer should be able to see pricing immediately.

Potential positioning:

> **No quote required. No sales call required. Choose your plan and start selling.**

## 20. Dealer Positioning at Launch

Do not initially try to convince dealers to replace Auto Trader.

The easier proposition is:

> **List here as well.**

At £49/month, the marketplace can become an additional acquisition channel.

A dealer does not need to cancel existing advertising.

The question becomes:

```text
Did this extra £49/month generate profitable enquiries or another sale?
```

Once the marketplace proves measurable dealer ROI, pricing power increases significantly.

## 33. What Not to Do Initially

Avoid:

- Vehicle-value pricing bands
- Too many private seller packages
- Expensive mandatory private listing fees
- Charging buyers for standard search
- Charging buyers for basic watchlists
- Charging for normal seller messaging
- Complex dealer contracts
- Too many microtransactions
- Commission on ordinary private car sales
- Pay-to-win search results

Keep the initial proposition easy to understand.


Paid tiers should not exist only because they generate revenue.

## 37. Configurable Pricing Architecture

Prices should not be hard-coded into application logic.

Create configurable commercial products such as:

```text
seller_packages
dealer_packages
boost_products
promotions
```

Potential package fields:

```text
id
name
seller_type
price
currency

billing_period
duration_days

listing_limit

feature_flags

visibility_weight

active

valid_from
valid_to
```

This allows pricing to evolve without redesigning the application.

## 38. VAT and Pricing Presentation

Consumer pricing should be displayed simply and clearly.

Private seller prices should display the full amount payable.

Dealer pricing may be shown as:

```text

```

or equivalent depending on final commercial/accounting treatment.

Current UK tax and consumer-pricing requirements should be checked before launch.

## 41. Core Commercial Proposition

The marketplace should be able to explain its private seller pricing in one sentence:

> **List your car for free. Upgrade from £9.99 if you want more exposure.**

And its dealer proposition in another:

> **Simple, transparent dealer pricing from £49/month — no quote required.**

The central commercial philosophy is:

> **Make the marketplace free and useful by default, then charge sellers and dealers when the platform gives them additional distribution, intelligence or measurable commercial advantage.**
