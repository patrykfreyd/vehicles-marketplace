## 5. Shared Pages

These should not belong exclusively to buyer or seller functionality:

-   Login / Register
-   Account
-   Messages
-   Notifications
-   Help
-   Privacy
-   Terms
-   Cookie Settings
-   Contact
-   Report Listing
-   Report User
-   Blocked Users

A person selling a vehicle today may be buying another tomorrow, so the
core identity should simply be:

`User`

with buyer and seller capabilities attached to the same account.

## Development Simplification

Although this structure contains well over 100 logical screens/views, it
should **not** become 100 independent page implementations.

Most of V1 can be covered with roughly **25--30 major page templates**
plus reusable components, tabs, drawers, modals and workflows.

Examples:

-   One **Vehicle Detail** template handles Overview, Specifications,
    Equipment, Performance, History and Pricing.
-   One **Advert Performance** template handles Overview, Funnel,
    Competition, Price and AI Coach.
-   One **Saved / My Garage** template handles Likes, Watchlist,
    Collections and Saved Searches.
-   One **Advert Creation Wizard** handles registration lookup through
    publishing.
-   One **Search Results** template supports Grid, List, Detailed and
    potentially Map views.

Plan development around **major page templates + reusable modules**,
rather than treating every function as a separate page.
